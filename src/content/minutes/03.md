## Key Takeaways & Decisions

- **Architectural decoupling & two-tier brokers:**
  - **Edge transport:** MQTT selected for lightweight, low-overhead device ingestion from vehicles/simulators.
  - **Event backbone:** Apache Kafka selected as the central durable event log, enabling replaying, stream processing and straightforward integration with analytical consumers.
  - Clean separation between the **real-time live path** (telemetry processing, WebSocket feeds) and the **operational path** (Core API, fleet management and batch analytics).
- **Telemetry simulation strategy:**
  - Since physical hardware (ESP32) is external, develop a realistic journey simulator emitting structured JSON (bike ID, timestamp, coordinates, battery, speed) early on. Demonstrating live simulated trips flowing through the pipeline provides tangible validation.
- **Problem framing & Event-Driven Architecture (EDA):**
  - Competitors offer closed, proprietary solutions. KINETIQ's key differentiator is open extensibility through events.
  - Adopt EDA design principles (drawing from IBM EDA patterns) to ground technical choices directly in concrete operational use cases.
- **Value-added modules & BI:**
  - Keep initial BI practical and user-centric — for example, a CO₂ savings credit/reward model and fleet health reporting.
  - Use InfluxDB as the dedicated time-series sink for high-frequency telemetry metrics.
