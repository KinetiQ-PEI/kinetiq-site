## Agenda

## Decisions

- **Time-Series Database:** **InfluxDB** selected for ingesting and querying high-frequency sensor telemetry (GPS, speed, battery, vibrations) efficiently.
- **Message Broker:** **RabbitMQ** chosen as the event broker for asynchronous message queuing, decoupled communication and telemetry distribution across services.
- **Backend Framework:** **Spring Boot (Java)** selected for core backend services, business logic, bike lifecycle management and REST APIs.
- **Mobile Application:** **Flutter (Dart)** selected to develop cross-platform mobile apps (Android / iOS) for fleet operators and users.
